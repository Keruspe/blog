It's available [there](https://www.imagination-land.org/files/gpaste/GPaste-50.10.tar.xz), get it while it's hot!

This release targets GNOME 50.

If you're looking for the legacy applet/tray-icon, it's available [here](https://github.com/Keruspe/gpaste-applet).

## What's new?

GPaste grabs seven global keyboard shortcuts, and until now the only way to get rid of one was to blank its accelerator, one key at a time. There is now a single switch to turn them all off, for anyone whose compositor -- or whose other clipboard manager -- already owns those chords.

Getting there turned up a fair amount of wreckage in the shortcut handling on both backends, so most of this release is that:

- New setting to disable the global keyboard shortcuts entirely
- Fix gnome-shell being left holding GPaste's grabs for the rest of the session, and GPaste giving up on them for good after a slow login
- Fix the daemon crashing on a shortcut activation arriving after its gnome-shell client was disposed
- Fix the global shortcuts under the portal: an edited accelerator never reached it, Super went out under a name it doesn't know, and a dismissed permission dialog was reported as a successful bind
- Fix the behaviour, images and history preferences pages acting on a settings change before their rows exist

And don't forget to run `gpaste-client dr` aka `gpaste-client daemon-reexec` after upgrading GPaste to activate new functionalities ;)

You can then run `gpaste-client daemon-version` to check that the correct daemon is running.
