It's available [there](https://www.imagination-land.org/files/gpaste/GPaste-51.1.tar.xz), get it while it's hot!

This release targets GNOME 51.

If you're looking for the legacy applet/tray-icon, it's available [here](https://github.com/Keruspe/gpaste-applet).

## What's new?

This release is mostly about making GPaste dependable: encrypted and SQLite
histories, storage migrations and passphrase changes now hold up against
failures, logouts and damaged files, passwords no longer linger on the
clipboard or come back in clear, and GPaste inside GNOME Shell behaves when it
is replaced or turned off. It also brings a few new features, from uploading
from the command line to deleting a history from the graphical tool's sidebar.

### New

- Strip or upload an item from the GNOME Shell menu's right-click actions
- Upload an item and copy its address from the command line
- Delete a history from the graphical tool's sidebar
- Offer to restart an outdated daemon an update left running
- Recognise passwords other clipboard managers mark as secret, and take them off
  on time
- Keep the last copy when its application closes, and passwords across a restart

### Fixes

- Fix the graphical tool's narrow layout, freezes on long items, and emptiness
  after a restart
- Fix how files, Unicode line breaks and GPaste's window icon are shown
- Fix GPaste inside GNOME Shell once replaced or turned off, and its questions
  on the lock screen
- Fix the migration and passphrase windows going blank, picking a storage or
  asking twice
- Fix the preferences saving what was not meant, and unsaveable settings looking
  applied
- Fix pausing the tracking being undone, and searching the overview starting
  GPaste
- Fix keyboard shortcuts on keypad keys, after a late portal, and their
  scrambled list
- Fix passwords and secrets lingering on the clipboard, in memory, in the
  history or in the log
- Fix encrypted and SQLite histories, migrations and passphrase changes losing
  data on failures or damage
- Fix histories switched, emptied, deleted or backed up wrongly, and copies made
  meanwhile lost
- Fix copied images, files, colours, spreadsheet cells, rich text and blank
  lines being lost or garbled
- Fix the memory limit removing items it should keep
- Fix adding a pipe, a device, a huge file or a name in another encoding
  breaking GPaste
- Fix uploads failing or losing the address, and uploading anything but text
- Fix gpaste-client crashing, hanging on its input or passing escape sequences
  to the terminal
- Fix rare hangs and crashes, and GNOME Shell freezing while a history is
  emptied
- Fix building without the systemd units or the extension, or with translations
  elsewhere

And don't forget to run `gpaste-client dr` aka `gpaste-client daemon-reexec` after upgrading GPaste to activate new functionalities ;)

You can then run `gpaste-client daemon-version` to check that the correct daemon is running.
